Infrastructure & Campus Facilities

Our campus is equipped with essential educational amenities to ensure comprehensive academic development:

Practical Laboratories: Well-equipped science laboratories for Physics, Chemistry, and Biology.
  • Science Laboratories: Well-maintained Physics, Chemistry, and Biology practical laboratories compliant with State Board norms and safety protocols.
  • Computer Center: Dedicated computer facility for Information Technology (IT) practical sessions, digital literacy, and internet-assisted learning.
  • Central Library: Comprehensive collection of prescribed textbooks, reference literature, competitive examination guides, and daily newspapers.
  • Sports & Athletics: Outdoor playground supporting volleyball, kabaddi, kho-kho, athletics, and annual intra-college sports events.
