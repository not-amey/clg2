About The Institution

Empowering students through accessible, high-quality higher secondary education since 1998.

History & Vision

Established with a vision to impart accessible and high-grade education to the youth of Malkapur and surrounding rural areas, Shriman G. R. Warange Junior College stands as a respected seat of higher secondary education in Shahuwadi region.

The institution offers full-time Class XI and Class XII coursework recognized by the Maharashtra State Board of Secondary and Higher Secondary Education. Our campus provides a structured academic environment supported by experienced faculty members, modern laboratories, and a well-stocked library.

Over the past 25+ years, the college has nurtured thousands of students who have gone on to pursue successful professional careers in Engineering, Medicine, Commerce, Management, Public Services, and the Arts.

Core Objectives

  • Academic Distinction: Consistent high pass percentages in Class XII HSC Board examinations.
  • Competitive Exam Support: Foundation preparation guidance for MHT-CET, NEET, JEE, and Foundation Commerce courses.
  • Character & Values: Emphasizing moral integrity, punctuality, and social responsibility through daily campus activities.
  • Inclusive Education: Providing equal educational opportunities and state scholarship support to rural students.
Campus Life & Discipline: Daily morning prayer assembly with students and faculty members at Shriman G. R. Warange Junior College, Malkapur.
