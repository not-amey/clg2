Admission Process & Eligibility

Admissions to Class XI (Science, Commerce & Arts) for the Academic Year 2026-27 are open to candidates who have passed 10th Standard (SSC) or equivalent examination from a recognized board.

Required Documents for Admission

  • Original 10th (SSC) Marksheet with 2 attested photocopies
  • Original School Leaving Certificate (LC / Transfer Certificate)
  • Caste Certificate & Validity (if applicable for reservation)
  • Aadhaar Card photocopy
  • 3 Recent Passport-sized Photographs

General Instructions

Prospectus and admission forms are available at the college administrative counter between 8:30 AM and 11:30 AM on working days. Seats across Science, Commerce, and Arts streams are allocated as per Maharashtra State Board merit guidelines and seat availability.
